# Northwind Analytics Product FAQ

Answers to the questions customers ask us most often. Last updated in January 2026.

## What does Northwind Analytics do?

Northwind turns data that lives in spreadsheets, CRMs and databases into reports that update themselves. You connect your sources once and design a report. Northwind then refreshes the numbers and sends the report to your team by email or Slack on the schedule you choose.

## Which tools does Northwind connect to?

Northwind has built-in connectors for:

- Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel (OneDrive and SharePoint)
- HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive
- PostgreSQL, MySQL, Microsoft SQL Server and Google BigQuery
- Stripe and Xero
- CSV files, uploaded by hand or sent by email

Anything with a REST API can be connected with the generic API connector on the Team and Enterprise plans. We review connector requests from customers every quarter.

## How often is the data refreshed?

Starter plans refresh every 24 hours. Team plans refresh every hour, and Enterprise plans can refresh every 15 minutes. You can also refresh a report by hand at any time.

## What are the plans and prices?

- **Starter:** £29 per user per month, for up to 5 users and 10 reports.
- **Team:** £59 per user per month, with unlimited reports, hourly refresh and single sign-on.
- **Enterprise:** custom pricing, with 15 minute refresh, priority support and a 99.9% uptime commitment.

Paying annually saves 15%. Prices exclude VAT.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every account starts with a 14 day free trial that includes all Team features, and no card is needed. At the end of the trial you choose a plan, or the account is paused and your data is kept for 30 days.

## Where is my data stored?

Customer data is stored in data centres in London and Frankfurt. Enterprise customers can choose to keep their data in the EU only. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never sell or share it.

## How long do you keep my data after I cancel?

You can export everything at any time. After you cancel, we keep your data for 30 days in case you change your mind. After that it is deleted permanently within a further 30 days, including from backups.

## Can I cancel at any time?

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stop at the end of the billing month. Annual plans can be cancelled before the next renewal. We don't refund the rest of a year that has already been paid, except where the law requires it.

## What support do you offer?

Email and chat support is available Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UK time, on every plan. We reply within 4 working hours on the Team plan and within 1 hour on Enterprise. Enterprise customers also get a named customer success manager.

## Does Northwind support single sign-on?

Single sign-on with Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID and Okta is included in the Team and Enterprise plans. Automatic user provisioning with SCIM is available on Enterprise.
